01 · Short-form · Instagram

A year of content for a campus startup

Picapool · Founding Member, Product & Growth · 2024–25

Picapool lets students nearby pool cabs, food, subscriptions and group buys. It had a small page and no budget, so reach had to come from the idea, the hook and the timing. I planned the whole year in advance, with every post, caption and release date. I made reels, jumped on new trends, and commented on viral videos to pull their viewers to our page.

What the numbers said

The two biggest reels were creator-led and told in first person; the best brand-voice reel was a price hook (half-price OTT). Memes got the most comments for their reach. So the brand works best as the answer to a familiar student problem, not as the subject of the post.

04 · How I work

From one idea to every channel

The same loop sits behind the reels, the films and the newsletter.

01

Plan the calendar

Release dates set around what the audience is living through: exams, fests, farewells, festivals.

02

Find the hook

A problem they already have, a price they already know, or a moment they're already in.

03

Shoot, cut, adapt

One shoot, several cuts: a teaser reel, a full film, Shorts and a post, each made for its platform.

04

Read the numbers

Views against follower count, and comments and shares against likes, then I adjust the next month's calendar.
